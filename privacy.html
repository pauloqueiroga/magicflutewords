Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Magic Flute Words is a free word game you play in your browser. There are no accounts and nothing to sign up for. This page explains what information is involved when you play, who can see it, and the choices you have.

In short

  • Your games are saved in your own browser, not on a server we run.
  • We use Google Analytics, which uses cookies, to count visits and see how the game is used. You can turn it off at any time with Cookie settings.
  • The game never asks for your name, email address, or any other personal details.
  • The game doesn't show ads, and we don't sell information about you.

Your saved games

The game keeps your progress - the stars you've earned and the guesses of an unfinished word - in your browser's local storage on your device, along with whether you've already seen How to Play and your analytics choice. This data isn't sent to us. Each browser, and each home-screen app, keeps its own copy. Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes it.

Transfer links

A transfer link carries your saved games (stars earned and any unfinished guesses) inside the link itself, and so does the link that brings games over from the game's old address. These links contain no name, email address, or other personal details. The game removes this data from the address bar as soon as the page opens, and we don't run any server that stores it. Because the data is part of the link, though, it can be seen by anything that handles the link. That includes the apps or services you use to send it, your browser's history and, in some cases, the analytics service described below.

Sharing results

The Share and Copy buttons on a finished game hand a short summary - the game number, its date, your stars, and a link to the game - to your device's share menu or clipboard. Where it goes from there is up to you.

Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand how many people play and how they use the game. Google Analytics sets cookies (named _ga, and _ga_ followed by an ID) and collects information such as the pages you visit and their addresses, how you interact with them, your browser and device type, and your approximate location. Google may process this information in the United States and other countries, under the Google Privacy Policy. Google explains more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If your device's time zone is in Europe, or hides your location, the game asks before loading Google Analytics and loads it only if you accept. Elsewhere it loads by default, but for visitors Google locates in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland it runs without cookies unless they accept. Google's advertising cookies and ad-related data use are switched off for everyone.

You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings, at the foot of the How to Play and Statistics dialogs. If you decline, the game removes Google Analytics' cookies and won't load it on later visits.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Other sites

The Ko-fi support button is an image loaded from Ko-fi's servers, so Ko-fi receives the information any website gets when your browser fetches a file from it, such as your IP address and browser type. Clicking that button, or the magnifying glass that looks up a word's definition, takes you to another site (Ko-fi or Google Search), where that site's own privacy policy applies.

Children

Magic Flute Words is a general-audience game and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Delete your saved games by clearing this site's data in your browser.
  • Turn analytics off with Cookie settings in the game, Google's opt-out browser add-on, your browser's cookie settings, or a content blocker. The game works the same without it.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights over personal data about you, such as asking to see it or have it deleted. To ask about any of this, get in touch as described below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue in the game's public GitHub repository: github.com/pauloqueiroga/magicflutewords/issues. Issues are public and need a free GitHub account, so please don't include personal details there.
