Magic Flute Words - a free daily five-letter word game

Magic Flute Words is a free online word game you play in your browser, with no sign-up and nothing to install. Each day you guess five hidden five-letter words, one at a time, using only valid five-letter words as guesses. Every word starts from three free clue words - MAGIC, FLUTE, and WORDS - which together reveal 15 letters of the alphabet. A new puzzle arrives every day, and you can play any past game.

A free daily five-letter word game - a new puzzle every day.

How to Play

Each day (in UTC - Coordinated Universal Time) brings five words to guess, one at a time. Type any valid five-letter word and press Enter to submit.

MAGIC, FLUTE, and WORDS are shown as free starter clues for each word. Together they cover 15 unique letters of the alphabet.

Stars

Earn up to ★★★★★ per word: first try gets five stars, each extra guess costs one. A perfect day is 25 stars.

Statistics

Tap the button at the top right to view your statistics: games played, stars earned, performance, and star distribution.

Past games

Tap the game number (e.g. #1 · Today) at the top left to see every past game and replay any of them. Your stars are saved per game in this browser - to move them to another device, use Transfer stats in Statistics.
